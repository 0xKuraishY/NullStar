The ranking philosophy

The score answers one question: if I only had ten minutes today, is this repo worth one of them?

That is why momentum is capped low and gem bonus exists. A proof of concept published this morning by an account created last week, with no stars and a sharp description, should beat a famous framework that merely got a commit.

Raise origin if you care mostly about vendor and Big Tech output. Raise gem bonus and lower momentum to push further into the long tail.
