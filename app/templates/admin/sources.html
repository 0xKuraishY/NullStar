How the sources work

search , the broad net. Topic queries catch tagged projects; intent queries ("proof of concept exploit") catch the untagged ones, which is where most zero-star work hides.

org , watches a publisher directly, so a Meta or Trail of Bits release is caught on the day it appears rather than when it trends.

starred , the sharpest early signal available: a respected researcher starring an unknown repo usually precedes everyone else by days or weeks.

user , follows one person's own output.
